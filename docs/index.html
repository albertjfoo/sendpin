SendPin

Send a destination from your iPhone to a Karoo 2, and navigate to it.

Look up a café on your phone, send it to your Karoo 2, and let the bike computer do the navigating.

Your phone broadcasts the destination over Bluetooth — much the way your power meter broadcasts numbers to your head unit — and the Karoo picks it up and opens its own Map Pin screen. About two seconds. No cloud, no account, no internet on the Karoo, and the two devices never even pair.

The Karoo 3 does this in its companion app. The Karoo 2 never got it.

Installing on the Karoo 2 needs a computer, once. Hammerhead's Companion app sideloading is Karoo 3 only, there is nothing on the dashboard for it, and the Karoo's own browser is not reachable from its launcher. This is true of every Karoo 2 extension, not just this one. 
adb install -r sendpin.apk
adb shell pm grant com.albert.sendpin android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION

That second line is not optional — Android 8.1 returns no Bluetooth scan results at all without location permission, and the app will look perfectly healthy while finding nothing. Granting it on the device when the app asks works just as well.

Using it

  1. Find a place in Apple Maps
  2. Share → Send to Karoo
  3. SendPin opens and broadcasts — leave it on screen
  4. The Karoo beeps and shows the pin, ready to navigate

The iPhone stops broadcasting by itself once the Karoo has the destination.

Worth knowing

  • Keep the app on screen while sending. iPhones hide Bluetooth service UUIDs from non-Apple devices as soon as an app backgrounds. An iOS rule, not something an app can work around.
  • The Karoo needs a GPS fix and the relevant offline map region. Indoors you get the pin and a “GPS required”.
  • One tap on the phone, one confirm on the Karoo. The pin screen asks before rerouting you — probably what you want mid-ride.
  • Google Maps is not supported. Its share links contain no coordinates at all.

The story behind SendPin

Often when I go on a ride, especially somewhere I don't know well, I look up a café or somewhere to eat on my phone. Even when I have a set route, I usually don't have that destination picked in advance; I just find a spot as I'm rolling through a town.

Which leaves me riding one-handed with my phone out, or trying to memorise the route and inevitably pulling my phone back out to check.

Meanwhile I have a bike computer I specifically bought for navigation. It's great, but its search experience will never match what I get on my phone.

So what I wanted was simple: look up a place on my phone, send it to my bike computer, and let the Karoo take it from there.

To be clear, this is already a solved problem on the Karoo 3, Garmin and Wahoo, via their companion apps. Just not on the Karoo 2.

Why I built it

I thought it would be a fun project, given how good the AI tooling has got, and because doing it over Bluetooth Low Energy seemed like an interesting challenge.

I should caveat all of this: I'm not a software engineer. I work in tech, but I was focused far more on what I wanted to solve than on how to solve it. At the end of the day, all that's happening is an iOS app sending a message over Bluetooth — much like your power meter does — carrying a latitude and longitude, and your Karoo reading it.

Given how niche this is, how much more I add to it is TBD. As of right now, it simply solves what I originally intended. If you have ideas or feedback, I'd love to hear them — leave a note ↗.

Installs SendPin 0.1. Tested on a Karoo 2 running firmware 1.613.2351.12; other firmware is unverified. MIT licensed.

Get the iPhone app

Coming to the App Store — requires iOS 17 or later.

  1. Download SendPin from the App Store
  2. Open the app and tap Allow when it asks to find nearby Bluetooth devices
Troubleshooting
SendPin isn't in my share sheet, or I can't find it
A newly installed app often isn't in that first row — iOS decides what goes there and an app can't put itself in it. Tap More at the end of the row to find SendPin. To pin it there for good, tap Edit in the top right and hit + next to SendPin to add it to your favourites.

Install Karoo Extension

  1. 1USB debugging
  2. 2Connect
  3. 3Install
  4. 4Permission

You'll need a computer for this. Installing talks to the Karoo over USB, which only works in Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer. Come back on one — or use adb.

  1. Settings → About, tap the build number seven times
  2. Back, then Developer options → USB debugging
  1. Plug the Karoo into your computer
  2. Hit the button and pick the Karoo from the browser's list

  3. On the Karoo, accept Allow USB debugging? and tick always allow. Keep its screen awake, or the prompt never appears.

Troubleshooting
The Karoo never showed the debugging prompt
Its screen must be awake. If you've connected from this computer before, the prompt is skipped on purpose — the key was saved the first time.
Nothing appears in the device picker
USB debugging isn't on, or the cable is power-only. Plenty of USB-C cables carry no data at all; try another.
Install failed with a signature error
An older SendPin signed with a different key is installed. Uninstall it on the Karoo first, then install again.
The device is being used by another program
Something else has claimed it. Close any other tab of this page — reloading isn't enough, the tab keeps its claim — quit Android File Transfer, run adb kill-server if you use adb, then unplug and replug.

Send Your First Pin

On the phone

  1. Find a place in Apple Maps
  2. Share → SendPin

    Optional — for faster sharing, tap More in the share sheet, then Edit in the top right, and hit + next to SendPin to add it to your favourites. It then appears in the first row every time.

On the Karoo

  1. The pin opens, usually within 5 seconds
  2. Confirm the pin — needs a GPS signal
  3. Tap Navigate to
  4. Follow the Karoo's turn-by-turn wayfinding

    Optional — turn on rerouting in the Karoo's navigation settings

Troubleshooting
Sending the same place twice does nothing
The Karoo ignores a destination identical to the last one for about ten seconds, so one send can't open the pin screen twice. Wait a moment and let it go through.
The pin appears but the Karoo says GPS is required
You will need a GPS signal to know where you currently are. Typically this is because you are indoors.
The Karoo shows the pin but not the map
You likely haven't downloaded the map region (e.g. California) for that area, and the Karoo caps routes at around 500 miles (800 km).
Nothing happens when I share from Google Maps
Google Maps isn't supported (yet).

Troubleshooting

Start with the status line in SendPin on the Karoo — it names most failures outright.

StatusWhat it means
Listening — green dotThe Karoo is fine. Check the phone was showing the SendPin card, unlocked and on screen.
Off — grey dotThe Enabled switch is off. Turn it on.
Location needed — tap to grant — red dotTap it and allow. Android returns no Bluetooth results at all without location permission, so nothing works until you do.
Waiting for Bluetooth — amber dotThe Karoo has taken the radio back, usually for its own sensors. SendPin keeps asking for it and recovers on its own; if it stays this way, restart the Karoo.

How to uninstall

  • On the iPhone — delete SendPin like any other app. The share sheet entry goes with it.
  • On the Karoo — Extensions from the main menu, tap SendPin itself rather than its Open button, then Uninstall.
